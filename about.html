Our Story

Born From a Vision of Accessible Elegance

Every great brand starts with a genuine desire to solve an everyday dilemma. For us, it was the realization that finding exquisite, modern jewellery often meant choosing between unaffordable precious metals or lackluster costume pieces.

Dazzle by Dua was created with the vision of making fashionable jewellery accessible to everyone by combining modern trends, elegance and affordability. We wanted to empower jewellery enthusiasts across India to freely experiment with contemporary styles, timeless motifs, and versatile silhouettes.

Whether you are curating an effortless look for college, accessorizing formal workwear, or looking for the perfect finishing touch for a weekend celebration, our pieces are designed to be loved, worn often, and cherished.

Our Mission
“Our mission is to make stylish and affordable artificial jewellery accessible to everyone while helping customers express their individual style.”

We believe jewellery is more than an accessory — it is an intimate expression of who you are, how you feel, and the moments that matter to you.

Transparency & Customer Protection

Important Product Information

Consumer Notice

“Dazzle by Dua exclusively sells artificial and fashion jewellery. We do not sell genuine gold, natural diamonds or genuine silver jewellery. Our products are fashion accessories and are not represented as precious-metal or genuine gemstone jewellery. Please refer to individual product descriptions for material and finish details.”

Materials & Plating

Our designs utilize high-grade fashion alloys, brass, stainless steel, cubic zirconia crystals, and simulated pearls. Each item features polished champagne, gold, or rhodium tone plating.

Jewellery Care Tips

To maximize the lifespan of your fashion jewellery, protect pieces from moisture, harsh perfumes, water, and sanitizers. Store them separately in dry pouches when not in use.

Complete Transparency

Every product listing on our website explicitly outlines its dimensions, weight, base metal, and finish, enabling you to shop with complete clarity and confidence.
